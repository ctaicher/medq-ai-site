Compare answer confidence with accuracy

A worksheet for finding confident errors and uncertain correct answers in a short practice set.

A learning resource from MedQ-AI, founded by Christopher Taicher, MD.

Record confidence before you reveal the answer. Over a short practice block, compare those ratings with what you actually got right. The point is to decide what to revisit while the reasoning is still fresh.

Use the same scale for every item

Choose low, medium, or high confidence. Reserve high confidence for an answer you can defend against a plausible alternative. Before opening the explanation, write one sentence naming the fact that drove your choice.

Choose the next exercise from the result

High confidence, incorrect
Write the assumption you relied on. Find the clue you missed or misread, then try another case where that clue leads to a different choice.
Low confidence, correct
Explain the answer without using the answer choices. Mark it for another attempt if your explanation depends on recognition alone.
Low confidence, incorrect
Identify a specific gap, review a short source for it, and try a fresh question later.
High confidence, correct
Save study time for weaker topics. Include an occasional unfamiliar case to see whether the reasoning carries over.

Use the numbers as a study signal

If you mark eight items high confidence and miss three, list those three items by the underlying assumption. A repeat assumption gives you a focused review topic. With eight items, the result describes this block only. Use several blocks before drawing conclusions about your overall performance.
