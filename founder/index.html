MedQ-AI founder

Christopher Taicher, MD

Emergency physician and author Chris Taicher founded MedQ-AI to help clinicians find their next useful study task after a practice question.

From answer history to a study plan

In a question bank, a score shows how many questions a learner answered correctly. The reasoning behind a wrong answer can be more useful for choosing what to review next. A learner may have missed a clue in a vignette, recalled the wrong rule, or applied a familiar rule in a different context.

MedQ-AI pairs board-style questions with a knowledge profile and curated educational material. For example, a learner who repeatedly misses questions that depend on the timing of a presentation can revisit that topic, then try to apply it in a different case. The question history gives the learner a starting point for the next study session.

Clinical training and teaching

Taicher completed the Harvard Affiliated Emergency Medicine Residency at Massachusetts General Hospital and Brigham and Women’s Hospital. He wrote Preparing for Residency about the practical work of joining a clinical team. His article in EM Resident examines how AI could help tailor learning to individual clinicians.

Carnegie Mellon and the project’s development

MedQ-AI was a graduate-track finalist in Carnegie Mellon University’s 2026 McGinnis Venture Competition and received a Gebhardt Sandbox Fund Prize. The project’s emergency medicine board-prep app is available for iPhone.
